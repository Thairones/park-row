About

Having collected dedicated members made up of all cinematic ideologies, hailing from the United States and across Europe, as well as Southeast Asia, our goal is to provide a collective space for a wide variety of people all interested in the perseverance of cinema. We hope to reach toward a different approach to film criticism. Thank you to all who choose to read our work!

— The Park Row Editors
